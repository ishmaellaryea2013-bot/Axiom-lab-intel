Everything you need to revise

One app, ten ways to learn. Every answer is marked the way a teacher would mark it, and you can see why you lost a mark.

🎧

Listen & notes

Notes for every subject, read aloud by a calm teacher-style voice. Choose a subject, a level and a topic, then listen or read along.

➗

Math practice

BECE-style questions that adapt. Get three right and they get harder. Worked solutions show how a teacher marks them.

📐

Geometry and graph labs

Draw, measure and explore shapes and graphs to see how the maths works.

🔬

Science labs

Interactive labs, including circuits and acids and bases, where you predict first and then test.

✏️

Draw cells

Draw and label animal, plant and bacterium cells. The app marks the parts, colours, labels and title like an examiner.

Science question book

Tables, differentiate between, life cycles, ratios, calculations, short answers and drawing questions, all marked with units and key words.

Learn French and Ewe

Short Duolingo-style lessons with cards, matching, typing and sentence building. Hearts, XP and a streak keep you going.

Daily exam

A new hard paper every time you come back, 100 marks, built from the topics you have studied.

Meet Intelligent

Intelligent is your guide in Axiom Lab. Every time you learn something new, a little more of the lightbulb glows.

  • Keep a daily streak and protect it with a streak freeze.
  • Finish daily quests and open a daily chest for gems.
  • Collect 16 badges and climb the weekly league from Bronze to Diamond.
  • Hear a cheer and see confetti when you do well. Sound can be turned off.

Get it on your phone or computer

Axiom Lab installs like an app, with its own icon, and keeps working without internet after the first visit. There is nothing to download from an app store.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app above, or the ⋮ menu and then Install app.
  3. Open Axiom Lab from your home screen.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open this website.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or Install the app above.
  3. Axiom Lab opens in its own window.

Good to know

  • Your progress stays on your device. Accounts and passwords are kept on the device and are not sent anywhere. The Gmail address is just an account name and is not checked with Google. Clearing the browser's data erases progress.
  • Ewe has no audio because phones do not have an Ewe voice. French audio needs a French voice on the device.
  • Some Ewe words should be confirmed by a teacher. Only the numbers, days and greetings were checked against sources.
  • Marking guidance is based on published WAEC paper formats and examiner reports. The exam bands (Excellent, Good and so on) are the app's own and are not WAEC grades.
  • Written answers are marked by key terms, so those marks are an estimate.
